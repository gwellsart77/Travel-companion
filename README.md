# Travel Companion (test version)

A phone web app for walking around a city: it nudges you when something good is close, keeps time on plans, and helps with phrases and quick translation.

This is an early test build. Open it on a phone at https://gwellsart77.github.io/Travel-companion/ and tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

- Uses your phone's location only while the page is open. Nothing is sent anywhere except map searches you tap, weather, and text you choose to translate (sent to the free MyMemory service).
- Personal trip plans are not in this code. They load on your own phone from a private link and stay there.
